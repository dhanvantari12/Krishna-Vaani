About Krishna Vaani

A Message from Krishna, for a Brighter You.

🪷 Our Purpose

Krishna Vaani is a free devotional website created to bring the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita closer to everyday life.

Our goal is simple: to help you begin your day with positivity, inner peace, courage, and spiritual inspiration.

📖 What You'll Find Here

Discover selected Bhagavad Gita verses presented with their original Sanskrit text, explanatory meanings, and thoughtful reflections.

Messages are available in English, Hindi, and Marathi so that more people can connect with these teachings.

💛 Free for Everyone

Krishna Vaani is free to use. No registration or login is required.

🙏 A Note About Our Messages

The Sanskrit verses are drawn from the Bhagavad Gita. The meanings are explanatory renderings, while the daily reflections are original interpretations inspired by the teachings.

These reflections should not be understood as direct quotations from Lord Krishna.

🌸 Our Wish

May every visit to Krishna Vaani bring a moment of calm, encouragement, and renewed hope into your day.
