Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: 26 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Krishna Vaani, a free website sharing Bhagavad Gita verses and reflections in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

Krishna Vaani does not require visitors to register or create an account.

The website does not currently request personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

3. Browser Storage

Krishna Vaani uses your browser's localStorage to remember preferences such as your selected language, favorite verses, and message history.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to Krishna Vaani through a user account or application backend.

4. Third-Party Services

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts. When external resources are loaded, their providers may receive technical information such as your IP address.

The website hosting provider may also process technical information necessary to deliver the website.

5. Advertising

Krishna Vaani does not currently display third-party advertisements.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain relevant cookies, data processing, advertising partners, and available privacy choices.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

7. Children's Privacy

Krishna Vaani is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website develops. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
