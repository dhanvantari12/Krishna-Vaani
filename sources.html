📖 About the Bhagavad Gita

The Bhagavad Gita is a sacred Hindu scripture presented as a dialogue between Lord Krishna and Arjuna in the Mahabharata.

Its teachings explore duty, self-knowledge, devotion, disciplined action, and spiritual wisdom.

🪷 Original Sanskrit Verses

Krishna Vaani presents selected Sanskrit verses from the Bhagavad Gita along with their chapter and verse references.

We aim to preserve the original wording and provide accurate references. If you notice an error, please let us know.

🌸 About Our Meanings

The English, Hindi, and Marathi meanings on Krishna Vaani are explanatory renderings intended to make selected teachings easier to understand.

They are not presented as official translations from any one publisher. Readers are encouraged to consult established translations and commentaries for deeper study.

💛 About Daily Reflections

Each verse is accompanied by a short, positive reflection inspired by its teaching.

These reflections are original motivational interpretations, not direct quotations from Lord Krishna or the Bhagavad Gita.

🙏 Respect for the Scripture

Krishna Vaani is an independent devotional and educational project. It is not officially affiliated with the publishers or organizations listed above.

Our intention is to share these teachings respectfully and encourage further study.
